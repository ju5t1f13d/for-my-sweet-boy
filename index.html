To my sweet precious boy

I am so thankful to have met you. You have brought me nothing but pure happiness. You truly make me feel seen and loved. I could never ask for anyone else in the entirety of the universe. Whenever I don’t feel like laughing, interacting, or doing anything at all - you make me want to do all the things that I feel unmotivated to do. You may not see yourself the way that I do, but I hope that you know that you have only had a positive impact on me. I love you very much, more than I can completely put into words. I never imagined myself loving someone as much as I love you. At first, I was more so scared about getting hurt again, but you have proven yourself in ways that have made me feel like I can completely trust you and never have to overthink things with you. I know I do overthink at times, but it is never because of you specifically and I’m sorry that it does happen. Your presence is so calming to me. I’m glad that we are able to spend so much time together either doing things together or just sitting in complete silence doing our own things. You don’t understand how much it makes me happy that we have so many things in common (kamen rider mention). In my eyes, I see you as someone who is smart, patient, funny, kind, attentive, and much more. You may not think you’re smart, but I do. There are aspects that you are full of knowledge on that I’m not. You are patient with me and I’m thankful for that. You genuinely make me laugh even whenever I don’t feel like it. I can also tell that you’re very attentive because I have noticed that you have adapted your ways of going around certain situations to best get me to communicate with you. I have never wanted to communicate my own feelings to someone as much as how I try to communicate them with you, even when I am shutting down. You are truly special to me, completely irreplaceable. Out of everything, I’m more so grateful that you chose me. We could have never spoken again, you could have had anyone else. However, you chose me. Even whenever I wouldn’t reply the best, you would still try talking to me and I find that really adorable. I’m more so a very avoidant person, but I genuinely feel like putting both of us in a bubble and avoiding everyone as long as I have you with me. I never have had to second guess anything, never had to stop to think or second guess anything with you. I feel very certain with everything when it comes to you. I love you very much. Happy birthday my sweet boy.
